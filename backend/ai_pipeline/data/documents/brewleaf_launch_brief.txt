1. ABOUT BREWLEAF

Brewleaf Coffee Co. is a small-batch coffee roaster founded in 2019 in Pune,
India by two friends, Aarav Mehta and Sana Qureshi. The company roasts beans
sourced from 14 partner farms in Karnataka and Kerala. Today Brewleaf runs 9
cafes across Pune, Mumbai and Bengaluru and ships coffee bags to customers in
over 120 cities through its online store.

The Brewleaf crew describes the brand as "coffee that takes its time." Every
product is made in small batches and nothing is rushed.


2. THE PRODUCT: BREWLEAF COLD BREW CANS

Brewleaf Cold Brew Cans are ready-to-drink cold brew coffee in a 250 ml
recyclable aluminium can. The coffee is slow-steeped for 18 hours in cold
water, which gives a smooth, low-acidity taste. Each can contains 120 mg of
caffeine, about the same as a regular cup of home-brewed coffee.

Launch flavours:
- Classic Black (no sugar, 5 calories per can)
- Vanilla Cloud (lightly sweetened, 60 calories per can)
- Mocha Midnight (cocoa and a touch of jaggery, 85 calories per can)

Price: Rs 129 per can. A 6-pack costs Rs 699, which saves about 10 percent
compared to buying single cans. First-time online customers get free delivery
on their first 6-pack.

Shelf life is 6 months unopened. Cans should be stored in a cool, dry place
and served cold.


3. LAUNCH DATES

The official launch date is Monday, 18 January 2027. Online pre-orders open
one week earlier, on 11 January 2027. Cans will be available in all 9 Brewleaf
cafes from launch day. A launch-day tasting event will be held at the Pune
flagship cafe (Koregaon Park) from 4 PM to 8 PM on 18 January 2027.


4. SUSTAINABILITY

The aluminium cans are 100 percent recyclable. Brewleaf has committed to
using 30 percent recycled aluminium in its cans by the end of 2027. Used
coffee grounds from all cafes are donated to local community gardens, and in
2026 the company diverted 11 tonnes of grounds from landfill.

IMPORTANT: Brewleaf's beans are currently NOT certified organic. The organic
certification process has started but is not complete. Do not describe any
Brewleaf product as organic in any marketing material.


5. CUSTOMER INSIGHTS

A survey of 800 Brewleaf customers in October 2026 found that 68 percent drink
iced coffee at least three times a week, and 54 percent said they would like a
ready-to-drink option they can carry to work or college. The top reasons for
choosing Brewleaf over other brands were taste (71 percent) and the feeling of
supporting a local, small-batch roaster (49 percent).


6. COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

Most ready-to-drink coffee brands in India are very sweet and use flavoured
syrups. Brewleaf Cold Brew Cans are positioned as the less sweet, more
coffee-forward choice. Brewleaf does not claim to be the cheapest option. The
message is quality and craft, not discounts.

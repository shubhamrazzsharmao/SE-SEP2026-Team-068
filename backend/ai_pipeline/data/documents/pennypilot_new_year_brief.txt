1. ABOUT PENNYPILOT

PennyPilot is a personal budgeting app founded in 2021 in Bengaluru, India
by two friends, Rohan Kapoor and Meera Iyer. The app has been downloaded
over 1.2 million times and has an average rating of 4.6 out of 5 on the
app stores. The PennyPilot crew is a team of 28 people working across
Bengaluru and Pune.

The brand believes that managing money should feel like a small daily
habit, not a stressful yearly project.


2. THE PRODUCT

PennyPilot reads bank transaction alerts on the phone and sorts spending
into categories automatically, such as food, travel and bills. All of this
processing happens on the user's device, and PennyPilot never asks for bank
passwords. Users can also set savings goals, called pots, and get reminders
before bills are due.

The basic app is free and allows up to 3 savings pots. PennyPilot Premium
costs Rs 99 per month or Rs 799 per year and adds monthly spending reports
and unlimited savings pots.


3. THE 30-DAY MONEY RESET

The New Year campaign promotes the PennyPilot 30-Day Money Reset, a free
challenge that begins on 1 January 2027. Each participant picks one small
savings goal and receives one two-minute tip every day for 30 days, plus a
short weekly check-in. Joining is free for everyone, including people who
do not use PennyPilot yet.

The first 10,000 people to sign up get 3 months of PennyPilot Premium at no
cost. After that offer ends, Premium is paid as usual. Participants who
finish all 30 days receive a digital completion badge.


4. KEY DATES

Sign-ups for the 30-Day Money Reset open on Monday, 28 December 2026. The
challenge officially starts on Friday, 1 January 2027, which is New Year's
Day. The PennyPilot founders will host a live question and answer session on
Instagram on Tuesday, 5 January 2027 at 7 PM IST. The campaign runs from
28 December 2026 to 10 January 2027.

5. CUSTOMER INSIGHTS

A survey of 1,500 PennyPilot users in November 2026 found that 72 percent
made a money resolution last year, but only 31 percent kept it through the
year. 58 percent said they lose track of their goals by February. The most
common reason, given by 64 percent, was not knowing where their money
actually goes each month. Users aged 22 to 35 are the largest group, making
up 68 percent of all users.


6. COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

Many money apps in India push investment products, loans and credit cards.
PennyPilot does none of these. It is positioned as the simple, calm, no-ads
choice that helps people notice their spending first. PennyPilot does not
sell user data and shows no advertising. The message is small steps and
awareness, not promises of quick riches.
